4. How we use your data

  • Newsletter and resources: to send you the guides, tips and updates you signed up for, and occasional offers, with your consent
  • Replying to you: to answer questions, book calls and follow up on requests you make
  • Improving what we publish: to understand which guides and emails are useful, so we can make better ones
  • Legal and compliance: to meet our legal obligations and resolve disputes

Signing up to the AI for Small Businesses newsletter only signs you up to AI for Small Businesses emails. We will not add you to the marketing lists of our other brands, such as Rebooked Beauty, without your separate consent.

5. Marketing emails and unsubscribing

We only send marketing emails to people who have agreed to receive them, for example by ticking the consent box on our newsletter form. We comply with Singapore's Spam Control Act 2007, and every marketing email we send includes a working unsubscribe link.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking that link, or by emailing info@bananadigital.co. We will stop sending you marketing emails promptly after you ask.

6. Text messages

We only send text messages to people who have explicitly opted in to receive them. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Mobile numbers and text-message consent are never sold, rented or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing.

7. Legal basis for using your data

  • Consent: when you sign up to our newsletter or agree to receive emails or texts
  • Contract: when we need your data to deliver something you have asked for
  • Legitimate interests: such as keeping our systems secure, preventing fraud and improving our content, where these are not outweighed by your rights
  • Legal obligation: when the law requires it

8. Who we share your data with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We only share it with:

  • Service providers that help us run AI for Small Businesses, such as our email and CRM platform (GoHighLevel / LeadConnector), our website host and cloud storage providers. They are required to protect your data
  • Banana Digital Pte. Ltd. staff and contractors who need it to run this brand, under confidentiality obligations
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers, under confidentiality obligations
  • Authorities where required by law or a court order

9. Transfers outside Singapore

Some of our service providers are based outside Singapore, for example in the United States. When we transfer personal data overseas, we make sure the recipient protects it to a standard comparable to the PDPA, or we put appropriate contractual safeguards in place.

10. How long we keep your data

  • Newsletter subscribers: while you stay subscribed. After you unsubscribe, we keep only your email address and the date you unsubscribed, so we can make sure we never email you marketing again
  • Enquiries that do not lead to working together: up to 12 months
  • Website analytics data: up to 24 months

After that, we securely delete or anonymise the data.

11. Keeping your data secure

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including password protection, access controls and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure. If you think your data has been compromised, email info@bananadigital.co straight away.

If a data breach is likely to cause significant harm, or affects 500 or more people, Banana Digital Pte. Ltd. will notify Singapore's Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) within three days of assessing it, and notify affected people as required by the PDPA.

12. Your rights

  • Access: ask what personal data we hold about you and how it has been used
  • Correction: ask us to fix data that is wrong or incomplete
  • Withdraw consent: stop marketing emails or texts at any time
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data, unless we have to keep it by law

Email info@bananadigital.co to use any of these rights. We will reply within 30 days and may need to confirm your identity first. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission in Singapore.

13. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tools to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are read most. You can control cookies in your browser settings. If we use third-party analytics or advertising tools, such as Google Analytics or the Meta Pixel, those providers have their own privacy policies.

14. Links to other websites

Our guides often link to other websites and tools, such as AI products. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data, so please read their privacy policies.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always at smallbizgrowth.ai/privacy, with the effective date at the top.
